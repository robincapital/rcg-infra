How to read this dashboard [close]

Two independent signal layers:
  • Fundamental upside (Target Px, Upside %, Composite) — long-term valuation thesis from price_targets engine. Updates daily.
  • Short-term technical (Pred score, Signal bars, Action label) — intraday momentum / mean-reversion from BBG bars. Updates every 30 min during market hours.
Action labels (technical, ~30min–4h horizon):
  • PRE-BREAKOUT · BREAKOUT · STRONG — bull momentum
  • WATCH · NEUTRAL — no conviction
  • WEAKENING · PRE-BREAKDOWN · BREAKDOWN — bear momentum
Combining the two layers for highest-conviction trades:

+WEAKENING / BREAKDOWNValue trap risk — wait
−WEAKENING / BREAKDOWN★ SHORT — overvalued + tape confirms
−STRONG / BREAKOUTMomentum chasing rich — wait for break
